# The Lamp Road

> "Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path." Psalm 119:105

An endless downhill snowboarding pilgrimage. One file: `index.html`. No images, no libraries, no build step.

## Play it

1. Open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari (double-click works).
2. Or serve it: `npx serve lamp-road` from the repo root, then open the address it prints (phones on the same Wi-Fi can use it too).

## Controls

| Action | Desktop | Mobile |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jump | Space / Up / W | Tap |
| Backflip | Hold in the air | Hold in the air |
| Cloak glide (unlock) | Press again and hold while falling level | Same |
| Pause / photo mode | P or Esc, then H | Pause button |
| Mute | M | Speaker button |
| Shepherd's Staff / Oil Flask | S / F | Item buttons |

Land flat to survive. Crashes scatter your sheep and wake the roaring lion. Let it catch you, or fall into a chasm, and the run ends.

Progress (best score, oil, level, goals, riders, items) is saved in the browser's localStorage.
